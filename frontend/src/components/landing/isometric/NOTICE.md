# Overflow Design drawings

the Browser, Kanban and Mail SVGs are derived from Overflow's free two-tone stills and used in Unified's landing page. theme colours, grouping and custom local motion have been added:

- Kanban: https://www.overflow.design/isometric/?drawing=240-kanban
- Mail: https://www.overflow.design/isometric/?drawing=240-mail
- Browser: https://www.overflow.design/isometric/?drawing=240-browser

source: https://www.overflow.design/api/mcp/
terms: https://www.overflow.design/license/#ai-coding-agents
retrieved 2026-11-12. the free stills may be included in a public project repository as part of that project. do not redistribute them as a standalone asset set. no Pro or animated assets are included.

`devices.svg` is adapted from the SVG supplied by the project owner on 2026-11-12. its colours and stroke attributes were adjusted to match the other illustrations. the original supplied drawing is used for the pull request stage.
